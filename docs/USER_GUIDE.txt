User Guide
==========
Typical day:
1. Clock in staff (Attendance).
2. Create or confirm bookings.
3. Check-in vehicle on arrival (Bookings or Queue).
4. Move wash stages on Live Wash Queue.
5. Record payment (Cash/Card/EFT).
6. Download receipt PDF.
7. End-of-day cash-up and reports.

Simple / Easy Mode (v0.4.0)
---------------------------
Use Simple mode for larger text and buttons. It is available to EVERY role
(including Owner and Manager — managers may also prefer larger controls).

How to turn it on:
- On the login screen, tick "Simple mode (larger text)".
- Or use the large "Simple mode" / "Full mode" button in the header or sidebar.
- On phones (/m), Simple mode is recommended by default.

In Simple mode you see: Home, Book a wash, Queue, Bays, Help.
Managers also get big buttons for: Prices, Today's money, Staff, Settings.
Bay status uses plain words: "Bay free" / "Bay busy".
When booking, confirm with "Yes, book it" or "Cancel".

Your choice is saved on your user account and remembered next time.

Cash vs salary deduction (v0.7.0)
---------------------------------
When booking via Quick Book, Easy Mode or phone (/m), choose how the customer will pay:

1. Cash — pay at the bay (no employee number needed).
2. Salary deduction — billed at month end. Anyone can choose this (including walk-ins)
   as long as they enter an employee number. Optional department can be stored.

When the wash is marked ready, salary-deduction bookings create a pending payroll row.
Managers export a CSV from Payments → Payroll export (default: current month), then mark
the batch as deducted/paid. Toggle “Allow salary deduction” and an optional monthly cap
warning under Settings.

Queue and bay cards show a CASH or SALARY badge. Receipts say “Cash – pay at bay” or
“Salary deduction – billed at month end”. Card/EFT/account/voucher remain available on
the full Payments screen.
